# As-Sabiquun landing page — comparison bar

Reference: https://www.augusthealth.com/ (captured 2026-08-28)

This is a **concept-demo** landing page. Any trust-strip marks are original fictional marks and must not be presented as real partners, endorsements, or accreditations.

1. **Floating navigation:** A white navigation capsule is centered with 24–32px inset from the viewport top, 60–68px tall, full pill radius, and one filled action pill at its right; it remains fixed while every content band passes behind it.
2. **Circular hero frame:** The opening screen centers a two-line editorial headline at roughly 64–82px desktop, with no more than 55 characters per line. At least six cropped circular/superelliptical media and interface fragments enter from the screen edges around it; the composition becomes a flat rectangular edge only after leaving the hero.
3. **Trust rail:** Directly after the hero, one concise heading sits above a single horizontal row of six detailed, monochrome, original fictional organisation marks. The rail has no cards, borders, or CTA.
4. **Six-card platform grid:** A small outlined eyebrow precedes a large serif heading. Six service cards appear as a 2×3 desktop grid, each with a pale tinted surface, 28–32px corner radius, a title and short copy at the top, an asset/graphic below, and a 44–48px circular arrow button at top right. Hover lifts the card 6–8px and shifts the arrow 4–6px right.
5. **Values well:** A dark teal/ink full-width section begins as a wide circular or superelliptical well and flattens to a normal band deeper in the scroll. It contains a centered white headline plus three large stacked, contrasting rounded statements connected by one thin decorative line.
6. **Case-study rail:** A large 16:9 rounded video placeholder anchors the section. Below it, a horizontally clipped row of three oversized story cards shows partial neighboring cards; each has a moving circular arrow on hover and compact dot/arrow controls below.
7. **Closing transition and footer:** A dark statement band with oversized outline circles flows directly into a two-column footer, with one high-contrast brand panel on the left and compact navigation on the right. The footer uses the same floating navigation above it, not a second header.

# As-Sabiquun `/contact` — Stripe Contact comparison bar

Primary rendered reference: https://stripe.com/gb/contact?topic=feedback, captured on 31 August 2026 at `1440 × 900` and `390 × 844`.

Stripe supplies the comparison standard for routing clarity, hierarchy, density, and responsive behavior. As-Sabiquun keeps its own identity, palette, navigation, truthful contact channels, service language, and project-support paths. No Stripe copy, icons, branding, product categories, or decorative assets may appear.

1. **The opening names the task before presenting choices.** The desktop title begins within the first third of the viewport, is roughly three times the body size, and is followed by one sentence of support. Both are left-aligned to the same content rail. Mobile preserves this order and shows the title, support, and start of the first route without a decorative preamble.

2. **Primary contact intents appear together and above secondary information.** Desktop presents all primary routes in one balanced row directly below the introduction. Mobile turns the same routes into one uninterrupted vertical stack. A visitor can identify the correct route before encountering addresses, safety notes, FAQs, or general company information.

3. **Every primary route uses the same compact decision pattern.** Each route contains one distinguishing visual mark, a short heading, no more than two lines of explanatory copy at desktop width, and one explicit action. The action remains anchored near the bottom edge so labels align across the row even when copy lengths differ.

4. **Each route offers one dominant next step.** Primary cards do not contain competing buttons. Action labels name the destination or outcome rather than saying “Learn more,” and button treatment is consistent across all routes. Secondary channels sit outside the primary card row and cannot be mistaken for equal-priority actions.

5. **The surface system makes structure visible without ornamental noise.** One pale page field carries the opening and routing area. White route surfaces use restrained corner radii, a soft downward shadow, and generous internal padding. One accent hue is concentrated in action controls and small route marks; large decorative shapes do not compete with the decisions.

6. **Secondary guidance changes density instead of repeating the card system.** Supporting routes appear below the primary row as lean text columns with headings and inline links, not another set of large cards. This creates a visible hierarchy between immediate contact decisions and useful follow-up information.

7. **Responsive behavior preserves decision speed.** At `390px`, navigation simplifies, the content uses a single readable column, route cards remain fully within the viewport, body copy stays at least `16px`, and interactive targets remain at least `44px`. There is no horizontal overflow, clipped copy, or breakpoint where two route cards compete side by side.
